A CPG assessment can include Sector-Specific Goals (SSG) depending on the assessment's sectors.

  • Chemical Sector (Not Oil and Gas) - organizations involved in the use, production, storage, or distribution of chemicals within one or more components of the Chemical Sector—including agricultural chemicals, basic chemicals, specialty chemicals, and consumer products
  • Information Technology Sector - organizations that develop software for internal, commercial, or open source use
